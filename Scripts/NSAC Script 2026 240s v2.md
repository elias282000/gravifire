## What changed vs the original (and why)

1. **Cold open (Option A — the certification trap)** (00:00–00:06): a spec sheet stamped PASSED on Earth against real SAFFIRE footage, landing the project's core question — *can Earth fire tests be trusted in space?* — in the first six seconds. The original stakes line ("losing time means losing lives") returned to its home at the end of the problem paragraph. The tagline hands off into the Riti story (00:35–00:40), the flow it was written for. The reserve trim was applied to pay for the longer cold open.
2. **Team member roll call kept spoken, verbatim** (team decision). To pay for the \~30 s it costs, two compensation cuts were applied: the spoken greeting moved to the title card, and the datasets paragraph's closing sentence was dropped (the lineage card carries it visually). One reserve trim remains at the bottom.
3. **Single-user framing.** "Engineers, researchers, mission teams" (three user groups — the study pack's anti-pattern) reduced to one user: the fire safety engineer certifying what is safe to fly.
4. **"Simulation" → "evidence engine"** (two places). "Simulator" sits on the weak-version list in the strategy deck; the actual mechanism is NASA experiment comparison. Also keeps the no-overclaim rule.
5. **PROOF beat added** (02:44–03:00): the blind hold-out result from the R1–R6 research track — 5 unseen NASA cases, 0 false SUPPORTED verdicts. This was the missing sixth pitch beat.
6. **Storyboard column added**: every beat now has a shot, a narration chunk, and on-screen text. Evidence lineage (dataset names, tc_ids, verdict stamp) is visible, per the lineage gap in the YottaByte postmortem.
7. **Team name: Yottabyte → NeuraVesta** (2026-10-06). Vesta = Roman goddess
   of the hearth and the in-game ship (ISV Vesta); "Neura" reads as neural
   and echoes the Bengali word for a firework fuse. Must match the team
   registration on the Space Apps site.
