**Part-03**

our solution is GRAVIFIRE, an interactive web-based simulation that turns NASA’s real microgravity fire experiments into an evidence-based decision experience.The journey starts with a Fire Safety Officer investigating a spacecraft material. The user selects a material and a mission condition, then compares what happened in Earth-based experiments with what happened under microgravity.Instead of simply predicting what may happen, GRAVIFIRE checks whether the existing NASA evidence supports the Earth-based assumption, contradicts it, or if the evidence is not enough to make a conclusion.

GRAVIFIRE is built on real NASA microgravity combustion experiments, including BASS, BASS-II, SAFFIRE-I, and SAFFIRE-II. These experiments provide the evidence behind our decisions and help us understand how materials behave in space.

This can help engineers investigate spacecraft materials, researchers study combustion behaviour in microgravity, and mission teams understand where existing evidence can and cannot be trusted.And when the available evidence is insufficient, GRAVIFIRE does not guess. It identifies the evidence gap and suggests what kind of next experiment could help reduce that uncertainty.

Now, let’s enter the world of GRAVIFIRE in the year 2038\. The ISV Vesta is travelling toward Mars. After a small fire incident inside the spacecraft, the Fire Safety Officer opens the GRAVIFIRE dashboard.From the Certification Queue, a material is selected for investigation. The user enters the Virtual Laboratory, compares the NASA evidence, and reaches a verdict: Supported, Contradicted, or Insufficient Evidence.And if the user asks why, EMBER helps trace the decision back to the evidence instead of making up an answer.
